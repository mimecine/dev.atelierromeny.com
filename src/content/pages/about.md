This site gathers the paintings, prints and drawings my father, Edlef Romeny, left behind: the work still with the family, in the atelier and the attic, photographed and catalogued so it can be seen again.

Inquiries

If a painting or print catches your eye, or you’d like to know more about one, write to me:

Email: marco@atelierromeny.com

Instagram: @marcoromeny

Mentioning the work’s title, or sending a link to its page, helps me find it quickly.

Many works haven’t made it onto the site yet. If you’re looking for something you don’t see here, a subject, a period, a technique, just ask, and we’ll find a way to show it to you.

## About the pictures

The works are shown as they are today. On some of the prints the mattes are marked, some sheets are foxed, creased or stained. However, all of the printed areas are in fine condition and only require a new matte. (Blame my father, whose storage was relaxed, to put it kindly.)

Colours on a screen can not quite match the real thing, and will vary from one screen to the next. The photographs are very close, but if concerned or wanting more details, please, ask and I’ll send more images.
