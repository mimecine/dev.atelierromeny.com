This site gathers the paintings, prints and drawings Edlef Romeny left behind: the work still with the family, in the atelier and the attic, photographed and catalogued so it can be seen again.

## Inquiries

Many of the works shown here are available. If a painting or print catches your eye, or you'd like to know more about one, write to me:

- Email: [marco@atelierromeny.com](mailto:marco@atelierromeny.com)
- Instagram: [@marcoromeny](https://www.instagram.com/marcoromeny/)

Mentioning the work's title, or sending a link to its page, helps me find it quickly.

Many, many works haven't made it onto the site yet. If you're looking for something you don't see here, a subject, a period, a technique, just ask, and we'll find a way to show it to you.

## About the pictures

The works are shown as they are today. Some mattes are dirty, some sheets are foxed, creased or stained, and a few paintings could use a clean: decades in attics and studios have left their mark. (Blame my father, whose storage was relaxed, to put it kindly.)

Colours on a screen can't quite match the real thing either, and will vary from one screen to the next. The photographs are close, but if colour or condition matters to you, ask and I'll send more pictures.
